Why should a writing test stop me from starting with a topic?

I recently tried to turn AI work into useful LinkedIn writing. Testing rules got in the way of beginning with a new topic or summary. That order made no sense to me.

I want to look more closely at the workflow and change its order. Controlled AI writing tests need boundaries. For an ordinary post, I want to start with what I have to say.

A rule that helps run a test can still get in the writer’s way.
