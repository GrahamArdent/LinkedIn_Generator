## Does the style meaningfully affect delivery?

- **Concise Challenger:**6 paragraphs,13 sentence-boundary units; approximate mean12.46 words. Compressed claim/contrast and a short second paragraph (“That is the distinction I would test in a payment system.”). More economical rhythm is observable, although its source paragraph is still dense.
- **Reflective Leader:**5 paragraphs,12 sentence-boundary units; approximate mean13.50 words. Opens from a leader's confidence and responsibility, qualifies the inference (“But I read them as a reason…”), and ends on responsibility. This is a change in interpretive framing, not just synonyms.
- **Technical Explainer:**5 paragraphs,10 sentence-boundary units; approximate mean16.80 words. Develops the dependency between recorded approval and enforced permission, gives a conditional mechanism, and puts the detailed scope limits at the end. The desired longer causal cadence is partly evident; approximate22-word guidance is not a quota or a passing score.

These counts use whitespace word splitting and punctuation sentence boundaries; they are descriptive, not validated linguistic scores. Different wording alone is not acceptance. All3 still use an early advisory summary and broadly similar claim→evidence→implication→verification reasoning. The shared argument/recommendation is intentionally controlled and is not itself a defect. The limited range of evidence and140-180-word envelope also constrain variation. This sample supports a modest style effect, not strong separation or general reliability. Do not expand to nine or keep tweaking automatically.

The three variables changed together before this batch: effective prompt projection, operational style definitions and a neutralized semantic objective. Within the batch only writing_voice differs (same frozen non-style hash). This is not a single-factor causal ablation against the earlier nine drafts. No claims that one specific repair alone caused improvement.
