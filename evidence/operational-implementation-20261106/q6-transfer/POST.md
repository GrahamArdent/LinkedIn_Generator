Packing a parcel faster doesn't mean it arrives sooner or in usable condition.

AI's first output is the packed box. Review and correction are still part of the trip. What matters is the effort needed to deliver work someone can use.

Stanford researchers studied 5,172 customer support agents. AI assistance increased issues resolved per hour by 15% on average. Less experienced agents improved in speed and quality. The most experienced saw small speed gains and small quality declines.

METR separately reported that some developers described differences in final work quality when AI was allowed, including code, tests and documentation. Neither finding predicts the result for every task.

Before calling a faster first pass a saving, how much work remains after it?
