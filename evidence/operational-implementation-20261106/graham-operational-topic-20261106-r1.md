If an AI gets the answer right, how much do you trust its explanation?

Anthropic found a gap in one addition task. Claude answered 36 + 59 correctly: 95. Asked how, it described the familiar method of carrying the 1. Anthropic’s analysis suggests Claude used a different internal strategy.

That finding concerns a specific task, and Anthropic says its tracing captures only part of the model’s computation. I wouldn’t use it to judge every AI answer.

For a consequential decision, I’d check the answer against independent evidence. I’d check the explanation too. A convincing account of the work is still a claim about the work.
