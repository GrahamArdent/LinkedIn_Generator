## Diagnosis
The order matters, but more calls alone do not solve it. The harness's shared freeze prompt asks for thesis, facts, qualification and narrative beats without requiring preservation of the route's selected narrative device. Route2's r2-s1.json contains selected_analogy key and image→mechanism→boundary→action. r2-brief.json replaces that with a conventional fact-first outline; r2-voice.json only sees the reduced brief. This is a demonstrated lossy handoff. A full editorial body also appeared prematurely upstream despite instructions. Repeated interpretation and long caveats then consume the available post length, leaving little room for voice or story.

The controlled voice experiment further freezes a four-part outline and extensive wording in the brief. That helps factual parity but may overconstrain expression. It does not prove that the catalogue voices are useless. The experiment conflates some early thesis choices with narrative choices, and uses one sample per method; it cannot rank all workflows generally.
